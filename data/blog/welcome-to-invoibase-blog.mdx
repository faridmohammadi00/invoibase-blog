# Welcome to the Invoibase Blog

Crypto payments should feel as simple as sending an invoice — without surrendering custody of your funds to a processor.

This blog is where the Invoibase team shares practical guidance on **non-custodial invoicing**, multi-chain settlements, stablecoin rails, and Web3 accounting for freelancers and agencies.

## What “non-custodial” actually means

In a custodial flow, a platform temporarily holds client payments, then pays you later. That introduces:

- Settlement delays
- Counterparty risk
- Chargeback and freeze exposure on some fiat rails

With **Invoibase**, invoices are denominated in USD for clarity, then settled **wallet-to-wallet** on-chain. Funds go directly to the wallet you control. We verify the transaction, send receipts, and keep your dashboard in sync — without taking custody.

## Who this is for

- Freelancers who invoice in USD but prefer USDC / USDT settlement
- Web3 agencies running retainers across multiple chains
- Studios that want branded pay links without building payment infra
